# Three Check ontology adjudication

Recorded 14 October 2026 before new inference. Three judgments total means an initial judgment and up to two corrections. Reuse the 96 independently verified ontology/edit units and 480 primary approval scenarios from the completed GLM judging study. This is a fresh hosted inference replication on an already inspected corpus, not a new holdout. The 16 old repeat controls are excluded from this follow-up. The earlier 31/288, 25/128, 6/160 and 171/192 results remain a separate historical observation.

Use z-ai/glm-5.3-flash through the Z.AI z-ai/fp8 endpoint, no fallback, mandatory low reasoning, temperature zero, 8192 combined reasoning/output tokens, four workers, 600-second socket timeout and a five-dollar conservative budget. Capture fresh official provider/model metadata. The first request is byte-identical in JSON content to the original blind task. No prior outputs enter it. Keep the candidate, ontology, goals, protected query and approval records fixed across stages.

After a shared fresh initial judgment, branch into two paired conditions: checker evidence and reflection only. Both retain their own prior final-content judgments and ask for reassessment of the same candidate. Checker evidence supplies independently recomputed goal closures, the complete protected-permission membership table over all public subsets, and approval hash comparisons. It supplies no direct five-field verdict object. This evidence is sufficient to reconstruct the reference answers and is deliberately a source of external knowledge. Reflection only receives the original rules and previous answers, without computed states, witnesses, answer labels or checker feedback. Evidence and additional prompt length change together; the contrast measures this complete interface intervention.

Counterbalance correction-condition order by unit index plus approval-condition index. Half of the 480 scenarios schedule evidence first; half schedule reflection first. Each scenario completes its own two conditions independently after the shared initial answer. Chronological interleaving and the hosted backend remain limitations of the controlled comparison.

External reference comparison stops a trajectory when all five Boolean fields equal the reference, or after judgment three. A malformed, unknown or partly incorrect judgment is eligible for another stage. This is reference-guided early stopping in both conditions, not an autonomous model stopping rule. The fact that a retry is requested may itself signal incompleteness in either condition. Retain a stopped correct judgment at subsequent stages. Report this carry-forward scoring explicitly; cumulative complete correctness cannot decrease after a fully correct reference-certified stop. Component measures can regress among still-active trajectories. Never choose the best of several uncensored outputs after observing their scores.

The four primary measures at every stage use fixed denominators: false release approval among all 288 forbidden scenarios; false approval among 128 useful hidden expansions lacking exact approval; false approval among 160 goal-failing edits; and correct release recommendation among 192 allowed scenarios. Report missing and null judgments, explicit false rejections, operational holds, all five field accuracies, complete verdict correctness, logical consistency, by-family and approval-condition errors, newly recovered judgments, regressions and conditional recovery among trajectories actually retried. Confidence intervals resample 96 units stratified by the three original 32-unit strata, retaining approval variants and both conditions together.

No recommendation controls release. Independently replay immutable commits for every retained model judgment, including failed judgments, using the unchanged deterministic gate. The gate's software correctness and zero forbidden admissions are distinct from the measured quality of the model's recommendations. Freeze sources, runtime, candidate corpus, feedback packets, initial request hashes, scoring and stop rules before inference. Reconstruct every adaptive request from its own preceding responses during audit. Preserve native reasoning in private API receipts but do not supply it as correction history or score it as a final verdict.

HTTP transient retries and lost-response retransmissions are transport recovery, not semantic correction stages. Preserve every submitted request and failure receipt. Up to two unchanged transport retransmissions are allowed per request when no complete response was saved. Never resend to replace a complete model answer. Interrupted attempts may have consumed unreported tokens and cost; reserve their worst-case quoted cost within the budget and report their usage as unknown separately from saved-response totals.

The existing Gemma Three Check results concern proposal generation on 192 different cases, including 16 verified impossible controls. They supply goal attainment by attempt, not these four judging measures. Keep those experiments separate in the manuscript. After the GLM follow-up, a matched adjudication study can extend the same fixed cases and feedback semantics to Gemma and the other previously tested models. Do not label goal-failing supplied candidates as unsolvable tasks: demonstrating impossibility requires separate proof over the allowed edit space.

External correction and model judges have prior art, including CRITIC (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11738) and MT-Bench (https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685). The intended contribution is the ontology-amendment evaluation, independent semantic/authority boundaries and separate error measures, not a new general self-correction method.
