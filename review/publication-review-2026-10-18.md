# Publication review, 18 October 2026

This is an internal review of Paper 2, the fixed Level 1 benchmark paper. It is not peer review or a claim that the draft is ready for submission to an unspecified venue.

## Defensible result

The paper provides a finite, executable diagnostic benchmark and descriptive measurements of six recorded hosted configurations on inspected development material. Its main study has 48 semantic DEV cases and 96 epistemic DEV packets per configuration, with six separate TRAIN interface checks. One judgment per case supports the reported point scores. It does not estimate variation between repeated generations. Twelve underlying epistemic DEV units generate the 96 matched packets, so packet or action counts must not be treated as independent sample counts.

This is a legitimate narrow empirical result. Bounded, static tasks are not a defect in themselves: they make the answer contract and exhaustive reference inspectable. The claims must stay within that contract and the sampled material. Near-ceiling Sol performance, correlated variants, inspected cases and provider-specific behavior limit broader claims.

## Remaining publication work

The paper is strongest as a transparent benchmark and diagnostic study, without a pooled leaderboard or general reliability claim. Ten fresh judgments per case would strengthen estimates of generation variability and case consistency; retain all trials and group correlated variants by underlying unit. Such repetition would not create new independent task units or a held-out evaluation. No repeated study has been launched or included as a result. The separate cost estimate covers ten fresh passes on the main six-configuration DEV set, not all historical cohorts.

Before submission: confirm competing interests, choose a venue and apply its reporting/template rules, obtain independent scientific review, and decide whether the intended claim calls for repetition or a prospective frozen evaluation on new units. This draft does not require Level 2 results to make its narrow Level 1 claim. Funding and affiliation have already been confirmed and do not need to be asked again.
